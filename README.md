# SBB Travelcard Calculator

A single-page calculator that tells you which Swiss public transport travelcard is cheapest for the way you travel: no travelcard, Halbtax, Halbtax PLUS (1000 / 2000 / 3000) or the GA.

## Use

Open `travelcard-calculator.html` in a browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.

1. Add the trips you make regularly: one-way price, how often, and whether it's a return trip.
2. Set whether the prices you enter are full fare or half fare, and how many weeks a year you're away.
3. Check the GA and Halbtax prices (defaults: CHF 3995 and CHF 190) and change them to match your offer, e.g. 1st class, youth or senior rates.

The results show the cheapest option, the yearly cost of every option, and how much more you'd need to travel before the GA pays off.

Your trips and settings are saved in the browser. Use **Export to file** / **Import from file** to back them up or move them to another device.

Prices are examples. Always check [sbb.ch](https://www.sbb.ch) before buying.
